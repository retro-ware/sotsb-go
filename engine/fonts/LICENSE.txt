The *.f8 files are the 8x8 code page 437 fonts of The Ultimate Oldschool PC
Font Pack v2.2 by VileR (https://int10h.org/oldschool-pc-fonts/), converted
from its Windows bitmap (.FON) versions to raw 256x8-byte glyph tables with
decomp/tools/fon2f8.py. Identical fonts were dropped.
